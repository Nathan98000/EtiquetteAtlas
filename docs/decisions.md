# Decisions

Decisions taken where the SRS was open or needed interpretation. Code comments and the method
notes cite them as "decision N". Decisions 1–14 were posed in `docs/m1-kickoff.md`; 15–23 were
added during M1 planning; 24–43 were agreed in M2 planning (`docs/m2-kickoff.md`); 44–56 in M3
planning (`docs/m3-kickoff.md`); 57–66 in M4 planning (`docs/m4-kickoff.md`); 67–77 in M5
planning (`docs/m5-kickoff.md`); 78–86 in M6 planning (`docs/m6-kickoff.md`); 87–104 in M7
(`docs/m7-kickoff.md`, whose open decisions 1–14 they settle); 105–110 for the public launch
(`docs/launch-kickoff.md`, whose open decisions 1–10 were agreed as recommended, and the findings in
`docs/launch.md`); 111 onward for the design changes before v1.0 (`docs/design-changes-kickoff.md`,
whose section 3 holds the maintainer's twelve decisions, cited as "design-changes decision N", and
whose open decisions 1–8 and the plan's recommendations 9–16 were agreed as recommended). Decisions
up to 77 were agreed by the maintainer on 22 October 2026, 78 to 110 on 23 October 2026, and 111
onward with the design changes' plan on 23 October 2026, as built on 23 and 24 October.

| 2 | Shrinkage SE and τ² | SE² = the cell's within-society variance pooled over societies ÷ n, not each society–cell's own SD (undefined at n = 1, zero when all agree). τ² = max(0, var of society means − mean SE²). |
