# v1.1 backlog

What v1.0.0, the public launch, leaves for the next version. The launch was a freeze: only fixes
went in, and Should items and improvements move to v1.1 (SRS §10). Each item names its requirement
or decision and why it waits. Findings deferred from the launch checks keep their ID from
`docs/launch.md`.

## First: the manual checks waived at launch

The maintainer launched v1.0 without these (decision 109); they are unmet parts of Musts, so they
come before anything else here. The checklists are in `docs/launch.md`.

| Check | Requirement | What stood in at launch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VoiceOver with Safari on a Mac and an iPhone, and NVDA with Firefox or Chrome on Windows | NFR-13, AT-6 | axe on 18 routes in both themes and the keyboard paths, on `build/` and the deployed site |
| A real iPhone (Safari), portrait and landscape | NFR-20, §2.4 | WebKit and an emulated iPhone 15 (`@compat`) |
| Each desktop browser's previous major version | NFR-20, §2.4 | The current versions, automatically and by hand |
| A human second reader for the method page | §9.4 | A fresh agent's review (decision 110) |

## Known gaps

| Gap | Requirement or decision | Why it waits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A `?society=` URL replaces prerendered text after mount, shifting the layout, on the audiences and on `/change/?society=…` | NFR-2, decision 31 | Prerendered pages cannot read their query string. Lighthouse's routes open without a society and stay at CLS ≤ 0.012; the fix is to reserve the text's space or prerender a neutral summary |
| A society's rank has no uncertainty | R-3, FR-25 | Needs a bootstrap of ranks in the pipeline, a new resampled quantity with its own seed key; the freeze leaves the pipeline alone |
| Dots crowd over Europe on the drill-down's map | FR-24 | Every society stays reachable from the beeswarm, the keyboard and the table; an inset or zoom is a new feature |
| Small differences on the fingerprint have subtle fills in the dark theme | §6.4, NFR-12, decision 46 | The palettes pass `colors.spec.ts`; ▲ ▼ and the details carry the notable differences |
| An audience effect has no interval of its own | FR-40, decision 60 | The two means share respondents, so their intervals cannot be combined; needs a paired bootstrap in the pipeline |
| Automated runs cover only each browser's current version | NFR-20, §2.4, decision 98 | As §2.4 now says: Playwright ships one build per engine, so the previous versions are checked by hand before each release (`docs/launch.md`) |
